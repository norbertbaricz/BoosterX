# 🚀 BoosterX

Welcome to **BoosterX**! 

Is your PC running slow, or do you want to get the maximum FPS out of your favorite games? BoosterX is a lightweight, easy-to-use desktop application designed to optimize your system's performance with just a few clicks. You don't need to be a tech expert or mess around with confusing Windows settings—we've made it completely safe and simple!

---

## ✨ Why use BoosterX?

* 🎯 **One-Click Boost:** Optimize your PC instantly without navigating through dozens of hidden Windows menus.
* 🎮 **Maximum Gaming Performance:** Clears up background resources, reduces system latency, and tweaks your PC for the best possible frame rates.
* 🧹 **Clean & Lightweight Interface:** A modern, simple graphical interface that is easy to navigate and doesn't consume your PC's resources while running.
* 🛡️ **Safe & Reversible:** Designed to be safe for your system. No extreme tweaks that could break your Windows installation.

---

## 📥 How to Install

You don't need to install any programming tools or complicated software. Just follow these easy steps:

1. Go to the **[Releases](../../releases)** page on the right side of this screen.
2. Download the latest **`BoosterX.exe`** file.
3. Place the downloaded file wherever you like (for example, on your Desktop).
4. Double-click the `.exe` file to open the app!

*(Note: Since this is a new custom tool, Windows might show a "Windows protected your PC" blue screen. Just click **"More info"** -> **"Run anyway"**).*

---

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Open **BoosterX**.
2. Browse through the available optimization options on the main screen.
3. Select the tweaks you want to apply (or just choose the recommended default settings).
4. Click the **Boost / Apply** button!
5. Restart your PC if prompted, and enjoy your faster, smoother system.
